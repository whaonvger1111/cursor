## 7) Reusable characteristics (falsifiable)

Each line is a **mechanism**. The 9/1 examples are illustrations, not a watchlist. “Would have disproved it today” is the test you run **the same afternoon**.

1. **If the bid is in non-index sleeves (seed / second-line retail / 短剧) while crowded hardware is sold, then the index can close red with majority advancers and ~0 limit-downs.**  
   *Today:* SSE −0.16, 3387/2040, 83/0 boards.  
   *Disprove today:* ChiNext and STAR50 close green, **or** limit-downs print in double digits while SSE is only −0.2%.

2. **If morning amount > prior morning but the afternoon dries, then the full-day SH+SZ print can still shrink — 11:30 is not the day’s volume verdict.**  
   *Today:* 三市午前 13612亿（+405 vs prior AM）→ 全天 20525亿（−934）. ChiNext lost the lunch bounce.  
   *Disprove today:* afternoon amount ≥ morning, or full-day SH+SZ expands.

3. **If yesterday’s crowded bounce was STAR/hardware, then today’s damage ranks STAR100 ≥ STAR50 > ChiNext > SSE.**  
   *Today:* −3.19 / −2.19 / −1.32 / −0.16. STAR50 gave back the entire 8/31 +2.24% plus more.  
   *Disprove today:* STAR50 outperforms SSE on a hardware down day.

4. **If the overnight object is a **spot/futures grain shock**, then seed/grain/农机 can board while hog (same 农林牧渔 label, different cash cycle) stays quiet.**  
   *Today:* 神农 +20、万向德农 +10 vs 牧原 +0.21、温氏 +1.19.  
   *Disprove today:* 牧原/温氏 limit-up with the seed board, **or** seed fails to board while hog rips.

5. **If the issuer prints a large **issuer-specific** bid (ESOP buyback), then the leader can go green on huge volume without lifting components or PCB materials.**  
   *Today:* 中际 +0.87 vs 天孚 −4.27、生益 −5.85、方邦 −11.06.  
   *Disprove today:* 天孚 and 生益 close green with 中际, **or** 中际 closes limit-down despite the 40–80亿 PDF.

6. **If a **national consumption opinion** lands overnight, then second-line retail/liquor can board while the mega consumer (茅台) does nothing.**  
   *Today:* 古井 +10、国芳 3 连板、茅台 0.00、五粮液 +0.79.  
   *Disprove today:* 茅台 outperforms 古井, or the retail board fails to seal.

7. **If the AIGC catalyst is a **named platform premiere**, then the platform and its 短剧 sleeve can hold limits while yesterday’s 30cm satellite and generic “media” fail to hold.**  
   *Today:* 芒果 20cm 再封、掌阅 2 连板 vs 流金 +5.65（冲高回落）、中文在线 +3.78.  
   *Disprove today:* 流金 closes 30cm and 芒果 fades off the limit.

8. **If the housing news is a **financing-rule rewrite** (closed-loop loans, no hidden local-gov guarantee), then banks/agencies can bid and developers need not board.**  
   *Today:* 银行创新高；保利 −1.41、蛇口 −1.23；我爱我家 +10.  
   *Disprove today:* developer sector board (≥5 开发涨停) while banks sell off.

9. **If a name has a **spectacular H1** but sits in a crowded, already-rallied factor, then the factor can still take the stock down on the next unwind day.**  
   *Today:* 长鑫 H1 印钞机数字 + 试产稿，收 −2.60 with STAR/memory.  
   *Disprove today:* 长鑫 closes ≥ +3% while STAR50 is ≤ −2%.

10. **If seal-rate is high and limit-downs are zero, that does **not** imply yesterday’s boards are being paid.**

## 8) One-screen checklist (reuse tomorrow morning)

1. **Index vs breadth vs boards:** write four numbers at 09:35 and again at 15:00 — SSE %, advancers, 涨停, 跌停. If they disagree, **do not** collapse them into one mood word.  
2. **Volume clock:** compare **this morning vs last morning** and wait for the afternoon before calling “放量/缩量”.  
3. **Same Eastmoney industry, two sleeves:** pick the pair *before* 10:00 (seed vs hog, CPO leader vs PCB materials, bank vs developer, 茅台 vs 二线酒) and record which sleeve the bid actually uses.  
4. **Catalyst class:** overnight PDF = issuer-only until the sleeve prints; national opinion = second-line first; commodity = the sleeve that **touches the spot**, not the whole 一级行业.  
5. **Held board vs +20% tease:** a name that was +20% at 10:30 and is +5% at 14:50 (today: 流金科技) is a **failed hold**, not a theme confirmation.
